My Uncle's Solar Panels (and why I changed my mind)

Honestly, I used to roll my eyes whenever someone brought up solar panels. Too expensive, too fiddly, and my dad always said they never pay for themselves. Then my uncle put some on his roof last spring, and I've been thinking about it ever since.

He's not exactly a "green" person, to be fair. He drives a truck that sounds like a tractor. But his electricity bill? Cut in half by July. Half! He showed me the app on his phone and I just stood there like, wait, really?

It wasn't all smooth, though. The install took three weeks instead of one because the guys kept getting rained out, and he's still arguing with the company about some paperwork. Also the panels look kind of ugly, I won't lie. My aunt hates them. We had a whole debate about it at dinner and nobody won.

Still, here's what stuck with me. On a really sunny afternoon he sent me a screenshot - his house was actually sending power back to the grid. His house! Selling electricity! I laughed so hard, then felt weirdly proud of him.

Would I do it myself? Maybe, someday, if we ever get our own place. I'd have to run the numbers first and I'm terrible at that. But I'm not rolling my eyes anymore, and that's something.
